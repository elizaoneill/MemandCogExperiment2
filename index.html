Memory and Encoding Strategies

A short study of how different ways of encoding information affect later recall.

Welcome

You will study 18 words using three different strategies. After a short counting task, you will be asked to recall as many words as you can.

Try to follow the instructions for each strategy. Words will appear one at a time. You will not be able to go back to earlier words.

This demonstration runs entirely in your browser. Your responses are not sent to a server.

When you are ready, begin the word list.
